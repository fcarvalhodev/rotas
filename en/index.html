The same standard on every route

Documented

The source documents the event or the statement. A record of an accusation proves it was made, not automatically that its content is true.

Open

There is an ongoing investigation, with no conclusion. Investigation is not conviction.

Allegation

A single-source account, message or accusation that has no proof yet.

  • Every fact links to the report or document it came from.
  • Claims can be True, Half true, False or Not shown. Interpretations and limited comparisons are identified; lack of proof is not proof of falsehood.
  • When the person cited has responded, their version appears alongside.
  • Both sides are held to the same standard, even when that weighs against one of them.
